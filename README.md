# Perfect Balance
Perfect Balance is a one-player abstract game where the player tries to get discs on 8 separate tracks to the center without going off the edge.

There are a total of 8 dice with 2 up arrows, 2 down arrows, 1 wild arrow (up or down) and 1 no move.

In a bag there are 32 tokens (4 of each color) and 8 tracking markers. 4 placed on space 3 (left) and 4 placed on space 9 (right)

On a player's turn they draw 2 tokens from the bag and place the tokens in an open space under 1 of the columns. This color token is now "tied" to that color row meaning any time that the marker in that row moves it will also move the "tied" token in the same direction. A column can have a maximum of 4 tied colors.

### Example: The player pulls out a Blue and Purple Token. The player places the Blue token in the spot under the Yellow Column and the Purple Token in the Blue Column

Players can place the same color token in the same column. Once place a token cannot be moved and it cannot have another token placed on top of it.

Once the player has placed the 2 tokens they roll the 8 dice. They now begin placing the dice, one at a time, in a selected column. They move the marker in the direction of the die and then move all tied colors in the same direction. The continue this until all the dice have been placed.

### Example: The player places an Up in the Yellow Column. The Yellow marker is moved one space up and, since the Blue Token is tied to the Yellow, the Blue marker also moves 1 space up. The player then places a down arrow on the Blue column and moves the Blue marker down 1. Since no colors are tied to the Blue Column (Blue is tied to Yellow but there are no colored tokens tied to Blue) that ends the movement for the Blue Column.

Once all dice are placed and movement is resolved the dice are collected, the player draws another 2 colored tokens and repeats.

The player wins if they can get all the markers in the safe green zone (depending on the level of play it could be at any time or after all Markers are moved)

The player loses if any of the Markers go off either edge
