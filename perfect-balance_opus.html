PERFECT BALANCE solo prototype

Turn 1 Bag 32 Safe 0/8
SETUP
Press New Game to begin.
Tuning knobs (the rules I had to pin down)
Off: a die only moves the column it's on + tokens directly under it. On: those moved markers also drag tokens under their columns (combos + cascade risk).
Instant loss: overshooting a marker past its safe end (e.g. a left marker above 7) ends the game. Allowed: it can sit in the out-of-bounds zone and you only lose at the physical edge (below 1 / above 11).
On: two Blue tokens under one column move Blue twice per trigger. Off: a color moves once regardless of how many of its tokens sit there.
Same seed = identical token draws & dice. Use it to replay an exact deal, or hand two testers the same number.
Difficulty variations (a–d) — apply on New game
Left safe becomes {5,6}, right becomes {6,7} — the outer safe row on each side disappears, so there's no margin against overshooting.
Each marker starts on a random valid row outside the safe zone (seeded, so a seed reproduces the same setup).
Drawn tokens auto-fill slots left-to-right, top row first — you lose all choice of which colour ties to which column.
Removes the wild and no-move faces. Every die forces a move; no relief valve, no flexibility.
